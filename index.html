About the Oddly Physics Tool

The Oddly Physics Tool is a private program used only by the operator of the Oddly Physics YouTube channel. It renders simulation videos on the operator's own computer, uploads them to the operator's own YouTube channel, and reads that channel's analytics. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
